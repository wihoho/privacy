PlainGrade privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy covers the PlainGrade Android app (“PlainGrade”), a gradebook, attendance and seating chart app for teachers. In short: PlainGrade does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • The classes you create: class names, student names and your private notes about them.
  • Assignments, scores, terms, grading categories and your letter scale.
  • Attendance marks and seating charts.
  • PDF and CSV reports you make, backup files, and your settings.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else. Student names and grades never leave the device unless you export or share a file yourself.

Export, backup, import and sharing

When you save a report or a backup, Android's own file screen lets you choose where the file goes, and the share sheet sends it to the app you pick. When you import a class list or restore a backup, Android's file picker gives PlainGrade only the one file you choose. Reports and backup files contain student records; keep them somewhere you trust and follow your school's rules for student data.

Permissions

PlainGrade requests no runtime permissions. It has no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Purchases

PlainGrade is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Storage and deletion

You can delete students and classes in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all its data (files you exported or backed up stay where you saved them). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Not an official record

PlainGrade is a record-keeping tool for teachers. It is not an official grading record, and we make no claim about compliance with any particular education privacy law; you remain responsible for how you handle your students' information.

Children

The app is meant for teachers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
