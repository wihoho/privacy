PlainDecibel privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy covers the PlainDecibel Android app (“PlainDecibel”), a sound level meter. In short: PlainDecibel does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Microphone

PlainDecibel asks for the microphone permission so it can measure how loud it is around you. The sound is turned into numbers (decibel levels) on your phone, as it arrives. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used while the app is open on screen.

Information the app keeps

  • Measurements you choose to record: one level per second, the date and time, the names you give them and any notes you type.
  • Your settings, such as the calibration offset, alert level and theme.

All of it stays in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Reports and exports

A PDF report or CSV file is created only when you ask for one. It is saved only where you choose (through Android's file picker) or sent only to the app you pick in the share sheet.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a measurement in the app deletes it from the app's storage. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Accuracy

Phone microphones are not calibrated instruments. Readings are estimates for personal reference, not a certified measurement, and the app is not a medical device.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information or audio.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
