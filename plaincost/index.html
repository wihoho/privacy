PlainCost privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCost Android app (“PlainCost”), a recipe cost and pricing calculator. In short: PlainCost does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Ingredients you enter: their names, what you paid and the package size, and optional cup or piece weights.
  • Recipes you enter: ingredients and amounts, how many a batch makes, labour time and rate, packaging, overhead and how you price it.
  • Your shopping list, your own units and your settings (currency, tax, rounding, theme).

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Sharing and backups

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to: when you share a cost report (PDF or text) or a shopping list, or save a backup file. It goes only to the app or place you pick in the system share sheet or save dialog. Backup files contain your recipes and prices unencrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Restoring reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

PlainCost requests no permissions. Files are saved and opened through the system's own dialogs.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a recipe or ingredient in the app deletes it from your data. Settings → Erase all data removes everything (a safety copy is kept in the app's private storage until it is replaced). Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Calculations

PlainCost works out costs and prices from the numbers you enter. It is a calculator, not financial or tax advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
