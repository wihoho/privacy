PlainLevel privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainLevel Android app (“PlainLevel”), a bubble level, angle finder and camera level. In short: PlainLevel does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no consent screens and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Accelerometer readings, while the app is open, to show the tilt. They are never stored, except a reading you choose to save.
  • Your calibration (your phone's sensor offset) and your settings.
  • Readings you choose to save, with the labels you type.
  • Photos you take with the camera level.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Camera

PlainLevel asks for the camera permission only when you open the Camera tab, to show the live picture with a horizon line over it. The picture is not recorded unless you press the shutter; a photo you take is kept in the app's private storage with its reading, not copied into your gallery and never uploaded. You can deny the camera permission and still use the bubble level, angle finder and notebook.

Sharing

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share it (a levelled photo or a CSV export of your readings), and only to the app or place you pick in Android's share sheet.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a reading in the app deletes it and its photo. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Accuracy

Phone sensors differ. PlainLevel is for everyday levelling after calibration; it is not a certified measuring instrument and not meant for engineering or surveying work.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
