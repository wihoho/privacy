PlainCoords privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCoords Android app (“PlainCoords”), a GPS coordinate converter. In short: PlainCoords does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • The coordinates you type or paste, converted on your phone into other formats.
  • Your location (GPS), if you allow it: your position, its accuracy and the GPS altitude, shown on the Here tab.
  • The places you save: name, coordinates, time and, for your own position, accuracy and altitude.
  • Your settings: the big format, distance units, theme and the last text you typed.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Location

PlainCoords asks for location only after you tap “Use my location” on the Here tab, and uses it only while that tab is open on screen. It never tracks you in the background and never sends your position anywhere. Converting typed coordinates works without location.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you tap a coordinate to copy it.

Sharing, maps and export

When you share a coordinate or export your places as a CSV, GPX or KML file, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick. “Open in maps” hands the position to your map app; if no map app is installed, it opens openstreetmap.org in your browser. Nothing is shared unless you do it yourself.

Permissions

Location (precise and approximate), only while the app is in use. No internet, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Purchases

PlainCoords is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Storage and deletion

You can delete saved places in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Accuracy

Conversions use the WGS84 datum. Your GPS position is an estimate from your phone. Check positions that matter for safety against an official source.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
