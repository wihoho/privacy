PlainVault privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainVault Android app (“PlainVault”), a calculator vault that hides photos, videos, files and notes behind a working calculator. In short: PlainVault does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Photos, videos and files you choose to hide, and the notes you write.
  • Your PIN and recovery code, stored only as salted one-way hashes (PBKDF2), never in readable form.
  • Album names and your settings.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else. Hidden files are protected by the PIN and Android's app sandbox; they are not encrypted inside the app's storage.

Choosing and removing files

You pick files in Android's own document picker, so PlainVault needs no storage permission. After a file is copied and checked, you may ask Android to remove the original; Android shows its own confirmation. Photos you take “into the vault” are taken by your camera app and handed straight to PlainVault.

Backups

Hidden files are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. You can make an encrypted backup file yourself (AES-256-GCM, with a password you choose). It is saved only where you choose, such as a memory card, a computer or a cloud folder you pick in the system file picker. We never see the file or the password, and cannot recover a lost password.

Sharing and unhiding

Files leave the vault only when you choose to share them, open them in another app, or unhide them back to your phone's storage.

Screenshots

By default the vault blocks screenshots and is hidden in the recent-apps view. You can turn this off in Settings.

Storage and deletion

Items you delete go to the in-app trash for 30 days and are then deleted. “Delete for good” and “Empty the trash” delete immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all its data, including everything hidden in it, so make a backup first. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Acceptable use

Use PlainVault only for content you have the right to keep. Do not use it to store illegal content.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
