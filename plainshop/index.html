PlainShop privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainShop Android app (“PlainShop”), a sales and inventory book for small shops. In short: your records are stored on your phone. They leave it only when you share or save a file, or when you turn on the optional Sync between phones, and then they are encrypted on your phone first. PlainShop has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Information the app handles

  • Items you enter: names, units, sale and cost prices, stock movements and low-stock alerts.
  • Sales: what was sold, quantities, prices, discounts, notes, customer names you type for credit sales, and payments.
  • Expenses and owner drawings, your shop name and currency, and phone settings (theme).

All of this is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage.

Sync between phones (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync, PlainShop makes a shop code on your phone. To reach your other phones, your shop's records (the information listed above) are sent over HTTPS to our sync server, PlainAPI, which runs on the developer's own computer. Before anything is sent, each batch of records is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your shop code. The code itself is never sent: the server only sees a name derived from it by a one-way hash, the time of each upload and the size of the encrypted data. We cannot read your records.

Anyone who has your shop code can read and change your shop's records, so share it only with your own phones and people you trust. If the code got into the wrong hands, use “Make a new code” in the app. If you turn sync off on a phone, that phone keeps its data and stops sending. To have the encrypted records deleted from the server, contact us (below) and we will delete them. Sync never blocks the app: when the server can't be reached, everything keeps working on your phone.

14-day server log

Our sync server (PlainAPI) runs on the developer's own computer. For every request the app sends to it, the server keeps a log entry with the full request and the full answer for 14 days. For PlainShop that is the encrypted batches of your shop's records your phone sends and receives, in the same encrypted form, so the log cannot be read either. Each entry also records the time, the app and its version or the AI model used, and the country and network part of your address (never the full IP address). It never keeps your shop code (the app never sends it), API keys or passwords.

Your encrypted records stay on our server for sync as described above; the 14-day log is an extra copy that is deleted on its own after 14 days.

The log is kept only to find and fix problems and to improve the quality of results. After 14 days each entry is deleted automatically, and the log is not included in backups. Only the developer can see it, in a private dashboard behind the developer's Google sign-in. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. To have your requests deleted sooner, email tmosby137@gmail.com.

Sharing, exports and backups

Your data also leaves your phone when you choose to share or save a file: a profit and loss report or receipt (PDF), sales, stock or expenses (CSV), or a backup file. It goes only to the app or place you pick in the system share sheet or save dialog. These files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

PlainShop asks only for internet access, which is used solely for the optional sync. Files are saved and opened through the system's own dialogs.

Storage and deletion

Settings → “Erase this phone's data” deletes all of PlainShop's records from the phone and turns sync off there. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone. Linked phones keep their own copy. Copies in the 14-day server log are deleted automatically after 14 days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We never hold your records back for payment.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
