PlainCount privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCount Android app (“PlainCount”), a countdown app with home-screen widgets. In short: PlainCount does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet, and it never asks for access to your phone calls, call log, contacts, location or storage.

Information the app handles

  • The countdowns you create: titles, dates, times, notes, stickers, colours, categories and reminder choices.
  • Photos you pick as a countdown background.
  • Your settings, such as theme and reminder time.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Photos

Background photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker, so PlainCount only ever sees the photos you pick and needs no storage or media permission. A copy of each picked photo is kept in the app's private storage.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Home-screen widgets

Widgets read your countdowns from the app's private storage on your phone to display them.

Backups and calendar import

A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and saved only where you choose. Importing a calendar (.ics) file reads only the file you pick; PlainCount does not request access to your calendar.

Sharing

A countdown leaves your phone only when you choose to share it (as an image), and only to the app you pick.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a countdown in the app deletes it and its photo. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
