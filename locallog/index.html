LocalLog privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026 (receipt photos; no purchases)

This policy covers the LocalLog Android app (“LocalLog”), a fuel, mileage and car service log. In short: LocalLog does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Vehicles you add (name, optional make/model and licence plate, units).
  • Fill-ups, services and other costs you log: dates, odometer readings, amounts, prices, stations and notes.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach to an entry.
  • Service reminders and your settings.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Receipt photos

When you add a receipt photo, LocalLog opens your phone's camera app or the system photo picker, and keeps only the photo you take or pick, in its private storage. LocalLog itself has no camera or storage permission and cannot see your other photos.

Backups, export and import

When you choose Back up now or Export CSV, the app writes a file (including your receipt photos, for backups) to the place you pick with Android's file picker (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app you have installed). When you restore a backup or import a CSV, the app reads only the file you pick. What happens to a file after you save it to another app or service is governed by that app or service.

LocalLog also keeps a few automatic snapshots of your log inside its private storage on your phone, so a failed update or a mistaken restore can be undone. They never leave the device.

Notifications

If you create a reminder with a date, LocalLog asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. It also re-arms them after your phone restarts. No notification content is sent anywhere.

Storage and deletion

Deleting an entry or a vehicle in the app deletes it from the log (older automatic snapshots on your phone expire after 7 days). Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information, and we don't use your location.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
