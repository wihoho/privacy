PlainStitch privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

This policy covers the PlainStitch Android app (“PlainStitch”), which stitches your photos together side by side, stacked or in a grid. In short: PlainStitch does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Photos you pick with Android's photo picker or file picker, or share to PlainStitch from another app. PlainStitch keeps a private copy of each photo you add, so your collage can be changed later.
  • Your collages in progress (layout, borders, colours and text you type) and your settings.
  • The finished collages PlainStitch saves for you (in Pictures/PlainStitch on your phone).

All processing (arranging the photos, drawing text and saving the image) happens on your phone. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Photo metadata

Saved collages are new images: they don't carry the location or camera details of the photos you used.

Permissions

PlainStitch asks for no storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permission. You choose each photo in Android's own picker, which gives the app access to that photo only. New images are added to your gallery through Android's media store, which does not need a permission on Android 10 and later.

Sharing

Images leave your phone only when you choose to share them, and only to the app you pick.

Storage and deletion

Your original photos are never changed. Deleting a collage from the Recent list removes it and its photo copies from the app. Uninstalling the app removes all its data (saved collages stay in your gallery until you delete them). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
