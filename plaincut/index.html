PlainCut privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCut Android app (“PlainCut”), an MP3 cutter and audio editor that trims, splits, merges, mixes and fades audio on your phone. In short: PlainCut does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Songs, recordings and videos you open with Android's file picker, or share to PlainCut from another app.
  • The files PlainCut saves for you (in Music/PlainCut on your phone).
  • A list of the files you saved (name, length, size) and your settings (default format and quality).

All processing (reading the audio, drawing the waveform, cutting, fading, merging, mixing, encoding to MP3, M4A or WAV, and saving) happens on your phone. Your files and the list are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

PlainCut asks for no storage, microphone, camera, location or contacts permission. You choose each file in Android's own picker, which gives the app access to that file only. New files are added to your music library through Android's media store, which needs no permission on Android 10 and later.

Only if you tap Set as ringtone, Android asks you to allow “Modify system settings” for PlainCut. PlainCut uses it for one thing: setting the file you chose as your ringtone, notification sound or alarm. You can turn it off at any time in Android's settings.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose to share them, and only to the app you pick.

Storage and deletion

Removing an item from the app's list keeps the file in your Music folder; Delete the file deletes it from your phone. Your original files are never changed. Uninstalling the app removes its list and settings (saved audio stays in your Music folder). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
