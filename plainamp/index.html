PlainAmp privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainAmp Android app (“PlainAmp”), a charging speed meter. In short: PlainAmp does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app handles

  • Battery readings from your phone's own battery sensor: charge level, current, voltage, temperature, charge counter, charging state and plug type.
  • Your charging history built from those readings, the names you give your chargers, and your settings (alarm level, units, design capacity).
  • Your phone's maker and model name, shown only in the app's Raw readings sheet.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on the charge alarm, the heat warning or the background watch, to show those alerts.
  • Foreground service and run at startup: used only if you turn on “Watch in background” (off by default). It listens to the system's battery updates so your alarm works and charges are logged while the app is closed, shows a notification while it runs, and restarts after a reboot. Turning it off stops it.

Sharing

Your history leaves your phone only when you export it as a CSV file and choose where to send it.

Ratings

After a few successful exports the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating card. That card is shown by Google Play, which operates under Google's privacy policy; PlainAmp sends nothing itself.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a charge in the app deletes it. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
