PlainShrink privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy covers the PlainShrink Android app (“PlainShrink”), a video compressor that makes your videos smaller on your phone. In short: PlainShrink does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Videos you pick with Android's photo picker or file picker, or share to PlainShrink from another app.
  • The files PlainShrink saves for you (in Movies/PlainShrink or Music/PlainShrink on your phone).
  • A list of the files you saved with their sizes before and after, small preview pictures of them, and your settings.

All processing (reading the video, compressing, trimming, extracting the sound and saving) happens on your phone with its own video encoder. Your videos and the list are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

PlainShrink asks for no storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permission. You choose each video in Android's own picker, which gives the app access to that video only. New files are added to your gallery through Android's media store, which needs no permission on Android 10 and later. If you ask PlainShrink to delete an original video, Android shows its own confirmation first.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose to share them, and only to the app you pick.

Storage and deletion

Removing an item from the app's history removes its preview picture; the saved file stays in your gallery until you delete it there. Your original videos are never changed. Uninstalling the app removes all its data (saved videos and audio stay in your gallery). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
