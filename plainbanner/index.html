PlainBanner privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy covers the PlainBanner Android app (“PlainBanner”), a scrolling LED text banner. In short: PlainBanner does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • The messages you type and the banners you save, with their style (font, colours, speed and so on).
  • Photos you choose as a banner background. The app copies the chosen photo into its own private storage so the banner keeps working.
  • Pictures and GIFs you export, and your settings.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Photos

Background photos are picked with Android's system photo picker, so PlainBanner only ever sees the one photo you choose. It has no access to the rest of your gallery.

Saving and sharing

When you tap “Save to Photos”, the exported picture or GIF is added to your phone's Pictures/PlainBanner album. When you tap “Share”, Android's share sheet sends the file to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Screen settings

While a banner plays full screen, PlainBanner can keep the screen on, turn the brightness all the way up and turn to landscape. These change only while the banner is showing and can be switched off in Settings.

Permissions

PlainBanner requests no runtime permissions. It has no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Purchases

PlainBanner is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Storage and deletion

You can delete saved banners in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all its data (pictures you saved to your Photos stay there until you delete them). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
