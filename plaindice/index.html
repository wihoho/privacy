PlainDice privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy covers the PlainDice Android app (“PlainDice”), a dice roller for board games and tabletop role-playing games. In short: PlainDice does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Your roll history: the dice or formula you rolled, the results and the time.
  • Rolls you save, custom dice you create, and your settings (sound, vibration, colours and so on).

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Motion sensor

If “Shake to roll” is on, PlainDice reads the phone's accelerometer while the app is open on screen, only to notice a shake. The readings are used immediately and never stored or sent anywhere. Reading the accelerometer needs no permission, and you can turn the feature off in Settings.

Permissions

PlainDice requests no runtime permissions. It has no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Purchases

PlainDice is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Storage and deletion

You can clear your roll history in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
