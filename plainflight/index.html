PlainFlight privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026 (notifications, maps, calendar)

This policy covers the PlainFlight Android app (“PlainFlight”), a flight tracker. In short: PlainFlight has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer receives none of your data. Your saved flights stay on your phone.

Information the app handles

  • Flights you save: flight number, date, airports, times, terminal, gate, seat, booking reference and notes.
  • Your settings.

This is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is included in Android's own device backup if you have that turned on (handled by Google under your Google account), and in backup files you export yourself.

Internet use

To show a route and a live position, the app sends only the flight number or radio callsign (for example “BA117” or “BAW117”) to two public flight-data services:

adsbdb.com – looks up the airline and route for a flight number.
adsb.lol – returns the current position of the aircraft flying that callsign, from a volunteer ADS-B receiver network.

If take-off and landing alerts are on, the app repeats this same request in the background about every 15 minutes, only around the times of flights you saved, so it can notify you. Nothing else is sent.

No account, name, email, device ID, advertising ID or location is sent. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when the app connects to them; they operate under their own terms. Your booking reference, seat, notes and other saved details are never sent anywhere.

Notifications

Take-off, landing and diversion alerts and check-in / time-to-leave reminders are worked out and shown on your phone. There is no push server: the developer never sees which flights you follow. You can turn alerts and reminders off in Settings, or deny the notification permission.

Maps and calendar

The route map is drawn on your phone from built-in public-domain map data (Natural Earth); no map service is contacted. “Add to calendar” opens your calendar app with the flight filled in; PlainFlight has no permission to read your calendar.

Clipboard

When you tap “Paste booking”, the app reads the text on your clipboard once to fill in the form. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

Flight details and backup files leave your phone only when you choose to share them, and only to the app or place you pick.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a flight in the app deletes it. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't use your location.

Not for operational use

Live positions can be missing, delayed or wrong. Always confirm times and gates with your airline. PlainFlight is not for operational or flight-safety use.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
