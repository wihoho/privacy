PlainFind privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainFind Android app (“PlainFind”), which helps you find your own Bluetooth devices nearby, such as earbuds, watches and tracker tags, by their signal. In short: PlainFind does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Bluetooth advertisements that devices near you broadcast: the device address, the name it advertises (if any), its signal strength and the maker or device type it announces.
  • The names of the devices paired with your phone, read from Android's Bluetooth settings.
  • Devices you choose to save: the name and note you type, when each was last heard and how strong its signal was, and the “It's next to me” calibration.
  • Your settings (beep, vibration, list options).

Scan results are kept in memory only while the app is open. Saved devices, calibrations and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Android 12 and later: BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT). Used to hear nearby Bluetooth devices, to show the names of your paired devices, and to read the live signal of a paired device that is already connected. The scan permission is declared with the “never for location” flag: PlainFind does not use Bluetooth to work out where you are.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only). On those Android versions the system requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan. PlainFind never reads, stores or shares your location.

PlainFind does not run in the background and does not scan when it is closed.

Other people's devices

A Bluetooth scan hears every device around you that is advertising. PlainFind is meant for finding your own devices. It shows what is in range at that moment and does not keep a history of other devices.

Storage and deletion

Removing a saved device in the app deletes its name, note and calibration. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Rating prompt

After you have found or saved a device a few times, the app may show Google Play's own rating card. This is handled by Google Play on your phone; PlainFind receives nothing from it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
