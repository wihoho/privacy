PlainCast privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCast Android app (“PlainCast”), which casts your own photos, videos and music from your phone to a TV on your Wi-Fi. In short: PlainCast does not collect, sell or share any data. It is free, with no in-app purchases, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers that the app talks to.

Information the app handles

  • The photos, videos, music and subtitle files you pick with Android's own photo picker or file picker, or share to PlainCast from another app. PlainCast can only see the files you pick or share; it has no storage or media permission.
  • TVs and streaming devices it finds on your local network: their names, models and local network addresses.
  • Your settings (for example slideshow speed and TVs you added by IP address), and the position you reached in each video so it can resume there.

How casting works

When you cast, PlainCast starts a small web server on your phone. It listens only on your phone's address on the local network, serves only the files you picked, each under a long random link, answers only the TV you are casting to, and stops when you stop casting or close the app. Your TV fetches the file from your phone directly over your Wi-Fi. Files are never uploaded to the internet or to the developer.

Subtitle files you add are converted to the formats TVs read (WebVTT and SubRip) in the app's memory and served to your TV the same way, only while you cast.

Photos are resized and converted to JPEG on your phone before they are sent, and the temporary copy is deleted when you move to the next photo or stop casting.

Communication with TVs happens on your local network only, using the TVs' own protocols (Google Cast, DLNA/UPnP and Roku's External Control Protocol). Like any device on your network, your TV's own software may handle what it plays according to its maker's policies.

Permissions

Internet (network access) is used only to find TVs on your local network, to control them and to stream your files to them. Network state is used only for the “Can't find your TV?” check, which tells you whether the phone is on Wi-Fi and whether a VPN is on. PlainCast asks for no location, storage, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

Storage and deletion

Settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone. Removing a TV you added deletes it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't upload your photos, videos or music anywhere.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
