PlainPlate privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy covers the PlainPlate Android app (“PlainPlate”), a photo food diary. In short: your diary stays on your phone. PlainPlate has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares data. The only thing that ever goes online is a meal's photos and note, and only when you ask for a calorie estimate (see below).

Information the app handles

  • Photos of your meals that you take or choose from your gallery.
  • What you write about a meal: notes, tags, the meal name and time, how hungry you were and how you felt afterwards.
  • The number of glasses of water you count, your meal names and reminder times, and your settings.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. Apart from calorie estimates (next section), none of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Calorie estimates (optional)

PlainPlate can estimate a meal's calories, carbs, protein and fat from its photos. This is off until you agree to it in the app. When you ask for an estimate, or after you turn on automatic estimates for new meal photos:

  • The meal's photos (up to 4) and its note are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the developer's AI server, which passes them to Google's Gemini AI model to get the estimate.
  • Photos are never stored: the server deletes them as soon as the estimate is returned. The text of the request (your note, without the photos) and the estimate are kept in the server's log for up to 14 days to fix problems, then deleted automatically. The log keeps only the country and network part of your address, never the full address. None of this is used for anything else, sold, or linked to you.
  • To keep the free limit of 20 estimates a day per phone, the app sends a random install id made by the app (not your device id, account or phone number). The server keeps only a hashed count per day for this, plus a hashed network address count against abuse, and drops both after 8 days.
  • The estimate is saved with the meal on your phone. You can change or clear it at any time, and turn automatic estimates off in Settings.

Google processes the request under its Gemini API terms.

Camera and photos

Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app and picked with the system photo picker, so PlainPlate does not request the camera or storage permissions. A copy of each photo you add is kept inside the app's private storage so it can't be lost if the original is deleted.

Reminders

If you turn on a meal reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Sharing, exports and backups

Your diary leaves your phone only when you choose to: when you share a day image, a PDF or a photo, export a CSV spreadsheet, or save a backup file. It goes only to the app or place you pick in the system dialog. Backup files contain your meals and photos unencrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a meal in the app deletes it from your diary (its photos are removed the next time the app starts). Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Photos sent for an estimate are deleted once it is returned, and the log of the note and estimate is deleted after 14 days, so nothing from your diary stays on our side.

Health information

PlainPlate is a personal log, not a medical device, and gives no medical or dietary advice. Calorie and macro estimates are rough guesses from a photo. What you record stays on your device under your control.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, and meal photos sent for estimates are not kept.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
