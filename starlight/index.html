Starlight privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Starlight Android app (“Starlight”, package com.wihoho.starlight), a chore chart for families: children finish quests, send a photo as proof, and a grown-up approves them for gold that buys wishes. In short: the family's data is stored on the phone. Three things leave the phone: the titles of quests and wishes go to our AI picture service so each one gets its own picture; and, only when a grown-up turns them on, family sync and Google sign-in send the family's records to our own sync server so the family's other phones can show them. Starlight has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and we never sell or share data.

Information the app handles

  • Children's first names (or nicknames) and, if a grown-up adds one, a picture.
  • Quests and wishes the grown-ups create, the quests a child sends (with an optional photo and a short note), approvals, wishes bought, gold and XP, the gold history, the weekly allowance, trophies and streaks.
  • The grown-ups' PIN, stored only as a salted one-way hash.
  • Settings that belong to one phone (who uses it, the sync state).

All of this is stored on the phone, in the app's private storage. Photos are kept at full size on the phone where they were taken.

AI pictures for quests and wishes

So that "Trip to Malaysia" shows an island and "Swimming lesson" a swimmer instead of the same gift box, Starlight sends the title of each quest and wish (and the quest's subject, such as "Swimming") over HTTPS to our AI service, run by the developer. It answers with an emoji and a colour, and draws a small sticker picture for the title. Nothing else is sent: no names, no photos, no notes, no gold or history, nothing about the child. A random id made once per install is sent with each request, only to count the daily allowance. Titles are written by the family's grown-ups and are used only to make the picture. The service passes the title to an AI model provider (Google) only to make the picture. The pictures are saved on the phone like photos and, with family sync on, shared with the family's phones. A grown-up can replace any picture with an icon or their own photo.

Family sync (optional, off until a grown-up turns it on)

When a grown-up turns on family sync (in the grown-ups' area, behind the PIN), Starlight makes a family code. To show the family on the other phones that use the same code, the records listed above are sent over HTTPS to our sync server, PlainAPI, which runs on the developer's own computer. Photos are sent only as a small copy (at most 800 pixels, about 40 KB); the full-size photo never leaves the phone. The server stores the records under the family code so the family's phones can read them, and keeps the time each record changed.

Anyone who has the family code can read and change the family's records, so share it only with your own family's phones. If a grown-up stops syncing on a phone, that phone keeps its data and stops sending. To have a family's records deleted from the server, contact us (below) with the family code and we will delete them. Sync never blocks the app: when the server can't be reached, everything keeps working on the phone and catches up later.

Google sign-in (optional)

A grown-up can sign in with Google in the grown-ups' area so that the same Google account finds the same family on another phone. Starlight asks Google only for the basic sign-in permissions (your name, email address and account id). The sign-in token is sent to our sync server, which checks it with Google and stores your Google account id and email address next to your family code. It is never used for anything else, never shared, and children are never asked to sign in. "Sign out" in the app forgets the account on that phone; contact us to have the link removed from the server.

Children

Starlight is made for families with young children and follows Google Play's Families policy. The child area has no ads, no links out of the app, no purchases, no sign-in, and never asks a child for personal information. A child can take or choose a photo as proof of a quest; it is shown to the family's grown-ups and kept in the child's journal. Names and photos are entered and managed by a parent or guardian, who can edit or remove them at any time (Family → the child → Edit → Remove from the family).

Permissions

Starlight asks only for internet access, used for the AI pictures and for the optional family sync and Google sign-in. Photos are taken with the phone's own camera app and chosen with the system photo picker, so Starlight does not need camera or storage permissions.

Storage and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Removing a child removes them from every synced phone. Records on the sync server are deleted on request.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't use the data for anything other than making the pictures and showing the family's kingdom on the family's own phones.

Changes
