PlainCV privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCV Android app (“PlainCV”), a resume, CV and cover letter maker. In short: PlainCV does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet, and it never asks for access to your location, contacts, camera or storage.

Information the app handles

  • What you type into your resumes and cover letters: your name, contact details, work history, education, skills and any other details you choose to add.
  • A photo, only if you pick one, and a signature, only if you draw one.
  • Your settings, such as theme, paper size and the last design you used.

All of it stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Photos

When you add a photo, Android's own photo picker opens and the app receives only the one photo you choose. It is cropped and stored inside the app's resume data. The app has no access to the rest of your photos.

PDFs, images, printing and backups

A PDF, JPEG image or backup file is created only when you ask for one. It is saved only where you choose (through Android's file picker), sent only to the app you pick in the share sheet, or sent to the printer you choose in Android's print dialog. Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup, so your resumes can come back on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account under their terms; the developer never receives it.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a resume in the app deletes it from the app's storage. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't use your location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
