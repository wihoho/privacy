Read faster with bold word starts, on any web page

FocusRead emphasizes the first letters of every word, right on the page you're reading. The bold start gives your eyes an anchor, so many readers lose their place less often.

What it does

  • Works in placeThe page keeps its layout, links and images. No reader view, no reload.
  • InstantPress Alt+B or click the icon; even very long pages change in a fraction of a second.
  • Your sitesAlways on for the sites you choose, or for every site.
  • AdjustableEmphasis strength and an optional fade, with a live preview.
  • Paste readerPaste text from documents, PDFs or email and read it with fixation points.
  • PrivateRuns entirely in your browser. No account, ads or tracking.

Yes. It follows dynamic pages and stays fast on very long pages.
