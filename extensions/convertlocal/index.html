What it does

  • Images → PDFJPG, PNG, WebP and GIF photos and scans. Phone photos come out upright, on image-sized, A4 or Letter pages.
  • PDF → WordHeadings, paragraphs, bullet lists and bold or italic text, as .docx.
  • PDF → imagesEvery page or the pages you choose, as PNG or JPG at 72, 150 or 300 dpi, with a one-click ZIP.
  • Merge and splitMerge PDFs in any order, or extract pages like “1-3, 5, 8-”.
  • Shrink scansMake scanned PDFs small enough for email.
  • Quick accessRight-click an image to convert it to PDF, or a PDF link to convert it to Word.

Questions

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. ConvertLocal converts files on your computer, inside Chrome.

Can it convert scanned PDFs to Word?

Not yet. PDF to Word needs selectable text; scanned PDFs need OCR, which isn't included yet.
