Questions

Is FairFill really free?

Yes. Saving and printing are always free, with no watermark, no account and no credit card.

Do my PDFs get uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Are FairFill signatures legally valid?

They are simple electronic signatures, which suit most everyday forms. For documents that need a certified digital signature or notarization, use the method your recipient requires.

Can I fill a PDF that has no form fields?

Yes. Click anywhere to add text, dates, checkmarks or a signature.

Chrome can already fill PDF forms. Why FairFill?

Chrome's viewer fills form fields. FairFill also lets you type anywhere on flat PDFs, sign with a photo of your real signature or a handwriting font, reuse saved signatures, whiteout mistakes and flatten the result.
