How to convert a PDF to Word without uploading it

Quick answer: Install the free ConvertLocal extension, open it, choose PDF → Word and drop in your PDF. It builds a .docx with headings, paragraphs, lists and bold or italic text on your computer, and saves it to your Downloads folder. The PDF is never uploaded.

Most free PDF-to-Word sites upload your document to their servers. That's a problem for contracts, payslips, medical letters or anything personal. ConvertLocal does the conversion inside Chrome instead.

Step by step

  1. Add ConvertLocal to Chrome

    Install ConvertLocal from the Chrome Web Store. It's free with no account.

  2. Choose PDF → Word

    Click the ConvertLocal icon and pick PDF → Word.

  3. Drop in the PDF

    Drag the file onto the page or click to choose it. A PDF link on a website? Right-click it and choose “Convert this PDF to Word with ConvertLocal”.

  4. Download the .docx

    The Word file is saved to your Downloads folder, ready to open in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Tips

  • The same tool turns photos and scans into a PDF, splits or merges PDFs, and shrinks scanned PDFs for email.

Other ways to do it

Microsoft Word can open many PDFs directly (File → Open), and Google Docs can convert PDFs you upload to Drive. Both are good options if you already use them and don't mind the upload.

Questions

Can it convert scanned PDFs?

Not yet. PDF to Word needs selectable text; scanned PDFs need OCR, which isn't included yet.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The conversion runs on your computer, inside Chrome.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no account.
