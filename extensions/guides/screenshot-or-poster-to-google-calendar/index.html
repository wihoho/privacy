How to add an event from a screenshot or poster to Google Calendar

Quick answer: Install the free NanoEvent extension, right-click the image (or use its crop tool to select part of the page) and choose the NanoEvent item. It reads the title, date, time and place from the image; check them and click Add to Google Calendar.

Event details often come as pictures: a poster shared in a group chat, a class timetable, a ticket confirmation page. Typing them into Calendar is slow and easy to get wrong.

Step by step

  1. Add NanoEvent to Chrome

    Install NanoEvent from the Chrome Web Store. It's free with no account.

  2. Open the image in Chrome

    View the poster, screenshot or page in a Chrome tab, for example in Gmail, Google Photos or on a website.

  3. Right-click the image or crop

    Right-click the image and choose the NanoEvent image option, or click the NanoEvent icon, start the crop tool and drag over the part of the page with the event.

  4. Check the details

    NanoEvent fills in the title, date, time and location. Several events in one image are listed separately.

  5. Add it

    Click Add to Google Calendar, or export to Outlook on the web or an .ics file.

Tips

  • Wrong year or time zone? Type a fix in plain words, like “it's in 2027” or “7 PM London time”.

Other ways to do it

Google Lens can copy text from an image, which you then paste into a new Calendar event by hand.
