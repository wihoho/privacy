How to combine JPG photos into one PDF without uploading them

Quick answer: Install the free ConvertLocal extension, choose Images → PDF, drop in your photos, put them in order and click Convert. You get one PDF in Downloads/ConvertLocal, made on your computer.

Sending a set of receipts, an ID card or a handwritten assignment usually means turning photos into one PDF. Online converters upload your photos to a server, which is a bad idea for IDs and documents.

Step by step

  1. Add ConvertLocal to Chrome

    Install ConvertLocal from the Chrome Web Store. It's free with no account.

  2. Choose Images → PDF

    Click the ConvertLocal icon and pick Images → PDF. JPG, PNG, iPhone HEIC, WebP and GIF all work.

  3. Add and order the photos

    Drop the photos onto the page, then use ↑ and ↓ to put them in order.

  4. Pick the page size

    Choose image-sized pages, A4 or Letter. Phone photos are turned upright automatically.

  5. Convert

    Click Convert. The PDF is saved to the ConvertLocal folder inside Downloads.

Tips

  • Is the PDF too large for email? Use Shrink scanned PDF on it.
  • Right-click an image on a web page and choose “Convert image to PDF with ConvertLocal”.

Other ways to do it

On Windows you can print images to “Microsoft Print to PDF”, and on a Mac, Preview can export several images as one PDF. On a phone, the Files (iPhone) or Drive (Android) scanner makes PDFs too.
