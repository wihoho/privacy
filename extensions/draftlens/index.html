What it does

  • Open DWG and DXFDrag a .dwg (R13 to 2018+) or ASCII .dxf file onto the page, choose a file, or right-click a drawing link and pick “Open drawing in DraftLens”.
  • Pan, zoom, fitSmooth with mouse, trackpad or pinch. Double-click or press F to fit the drawing.
  • LayersTurn layers on and off and see their colors.
  • MeasureMeasure distances with snapping to endpoints and centers, in the drawing's own units, with ΔX, ΔY and angle.
  • Save or printSave the current view as a PNG, or print it.
  • PrivateDrawings are processed in your browser and never uploaded. No account, no ads.

Questions

How do I open a DWG file on a Chromebook?

Install DraftLens, click its icon and drop the .dwg file onto the page. It works fully in Chrome, so no Linux apps or CAD installs are needed.

Do my drawings get uploaded?

No. DraftLens reads the file inside your browser with the open-source LibreDWG library. Nothing leaves your computer.

Can I edit drawings?

No, DraftLens is a viewer: open, navigate, toggle layers, measure, save as PNG and print.

What isn't supported yet?

Binary DXF, paper-space layouts, 3D solids, embedded images and external references.

Is it really free?

Yes. DraftLens is free and open source (GPL-3.0).
