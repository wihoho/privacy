What it does

  • Expands as you typeAn expanded keyword list appears right under Amazon's search box. Click any keyword to search it.
  • Research pageSeveral seed keywords at once, a department filter (Books, Kindle, Clothing…), standard or deep expansion.
  • Exclude wordsHide competitor brands or terms you don't sell.

Questions

Is SuggestScope free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Where does the keyword data come from?

From the same suggestion data Amazon's own search box loads, requested at a gentle pace from your browser.

Does it show search volume?

No. Its “strength” score is relative, based on suggestion rank. It is not a search-volume estimate.

Can I use it for Kindle Direct Publishing?

Yes. Filter by Books or Kindle and use the KDP helper to fill your 7 keyword slots.
