Privacy policies

Chrome extensions by wihoho. None of them collect, sell or share personal data.

ClearRec · Record your voice with no time limit. Crash-safe, private, and saves MP3 or WAV in one click.
FocusRead · Read faster and stay on track: bold the start of every word on any web page, in place. Fast, private, works offline.
FairFill · Fill in and sign PDF forms for free. Save and print without a watermark or account. Your files never leave your computer.
ConvertLocal · Convert images to PDF, PDF to Word, images or text, and merge or split PDFs, all on your computer. No uploads, no email.
DraftLens · Open DWG and DXF drawings in Chrome: pan, zoom, layers and measuring. Private: drawings never leave your computer.
SuggestScope · See every Amazon search suggestion before and after your keyword in 20 marketplaces. Export CSV, build backend search terms. No ads.
SpeakType · Dictate into any text box on the web: email, docs, forms and chat. Spoken punctuation, 47 languages, works where you type.
