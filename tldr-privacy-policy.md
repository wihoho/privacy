# Privacy Policy for tldr.ai Chrome Extension

**Last Updated**: [Current Date]

## Overview

tldr.ai ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

### Content You Choose to Process
- **Webpage Content**: When you click the extension icon, we temporarily access the text content of the current webpage to extract the main article content.
- **Page Metadata**: We may access the page title and URL to provide context for the AI summarization.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect personal information
- We do not store browsing history
- We do not track your web activity
- We do not collect data from tabs you haven't explicitly chosen to summarize
- We do not store extracted content on our servers

## How We Use Information

### Content Processing
- **Local Processing**: All content extraction happens locally in your browser
- **AI Studio Integration**: Extracted content is sent only to Google AI Studio when you use the extension
- **No Storage**: We do not store or retain any content after processing

### Permissions Usage
- **activeTab**: Only used to access content when you click the extension icon
- **scripting**: Only used to extract content and integrate with AI Studio
- **tabs**: Only used to open AI Studio in a new tab
- **notifications**: Only used to show status updates and error messages

## Data Sharing

### Google AI Studio
- When you use the extension, extracted content is sent to Google AI Studio for summarization
- This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
- We do not control how Google processes this data

### No Third-Party Sharing
- We do not share data with any other third parties
- We do not sell or monetize your data
- We do not use analytics or tracking services

## Data Security

### Local Processing
- All content extraction happens in your browser
- No data transmission except to Google AI Studio
- Secure HTTPS connections for all communications

### No Data Retention
- We do not store extracted content
- No databases or servers collect your information
- Content is processed in real-time and discarded

## Your Rights and Controls

### User Control
- You control when the extension accesses content by clicking the icon
- You can disable or uninstall the extension at any time
- You can revoke permissions through Chrome's extension settings

### Chrome Extension Permissions
- All permissions are used only for stated functionality
- You can review permissions in Chrome's extension management page
- Permissions cannot be used for any other purpose

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
