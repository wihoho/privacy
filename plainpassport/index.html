PlainPassport privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy covers the PlainPassport Android app (“PlainPassport”), which makes passport, visa and ID photos. In short: PlainPassport does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • The photo you take with your camera app or choose from your gallery.
  • Face positions and a person outline worked out from that photo, used to size the head and replace the background.
  • The document type you picked, any custom sizes you saved, and your settings.

Photos are processed on your phone, in memory and in the app's private temporary storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Faces. Face detection and background removal run entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit models, which are bundled inside the app. The app finds where your eyes, chin and head are so it can frame the photo; it does not recognise or identify who you are, and it does not keep any face data after you leave the editor.

Camera and photos

“Take a photo” opens your phone's own camera app, and “Choose a photo” uses the Android photo picker, so PlainPassport needs neither the camera permission nor access to your photo library. It only sees the single photo you hand to it.

Saving, sharing and printing

Photos leave the app only when you save, share or print them, and only to the place or app you pick. Saved files are written where you choose using the Android file dialog.

Storage and deletion

Working copies are kept in the app's temporary folder and are cleared when the app starts and when you tap “Clear temporary files” in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information, including biometric data.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
