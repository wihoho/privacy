PlainArea privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers the PlainArea Android app (“PlainArea”), a tool for measuring the area of land and the length of paths on a map. In short: PlainArea does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your measurements stay on your phone.

Information the app handles

  • The shapes you measure: the corner points you tap, import or record by walking, with the names and notes you give them.
  • Your phone's location, only while you use “My location” or the walk screen.
  • Your settings (units, map type, theme), the last map position and your recent place searches.

All measuring happens on your phone. Measurements, settings and searches are stored only in the app's private storage and are never sent to the developer.

Permissions

  • Location (approximate or precise): asked for only when you tap “My location” or open the walk screen. It is used while that screen is open, never in the background, and is not stored except as the corner points you choose to save. You can refuse and still measure by tapping the map or typing coordinates.
  • Internet: to download map images and to search for places by name.

Online services the app uses

To show the map and find places, your phone talks directly to these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the request itself; the developer receives nothing.

  • Map images: OpenStreetMap tile servers (street map) and Esri ArcGIS Online (satellite imagery and labels) receive which map squares are being shown. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and the Esri privacy statement.
  • Place search: when you search for a place by name, the text you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Coordinates you type are handled on the phone and are not sent.

Your location is never sent to these services by the app; only the map area on screen is. Map images you have viewed are kept in the phone's cache so they show faster next time.

Sharing

Measurements leave your phone only when you choose to share or export them (as an image, KML, GPX, GeoJSON, CSV or text), and only to the app or place you pick. Files you import are read on the phone.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a measurement in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information, including location.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services, and the app sends no notifications.

Accuracy

Phone GPS and map imagery are accurate to a few metres. Measurements are estimates and are not a legal survey.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
