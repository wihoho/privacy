PlainSpeak privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainSpeak Android app (“PlainSpeak”), a talking picture-card (AAC) app. In short: PlainSpeak does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Your board: the cards and folders, their words, colours and pictures, including photos you add to cards.
  • Favourite phrases and the sentences you said recently.
  • Your settings, such as speech speed, pitch and voice.

All of this stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Speech

PlainSpeak speaks with the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's or Samsung's). The words are handed to that engine on your device; PlainSpeak itself sends nothing anywhere. Your speech engine's own settings and policy decide whether it uses the network; most voices work offline.

Photos, backup and restore

When you add a photo to a card, Android's photo picker or camera gives PlainSpeak only the one picture you choose, and a copy is kept in the app's private storage. When you back up, Android's own file screen lets you choose where the file goes; when you restore, it gives PlainSpeak only the file you pick. Backup files contain your board, phrases and photos; keep them somewhere you trust.

Permissions

PlainSpeak requests no runtime permissions. It has no internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera or storage permission (taking a photo for a card is done by your camera app).

Purchases

PlainSpeak has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Children

PlainSpeak is used by children and adults, usually set up by a parent or carer. It collects no information from anyone, shows no ads and has no accounts. Settings, editing and the link to this page sit behind a simple adult check.

Storage and deletion

You can delete cards and phrases in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all its data (backup files you saved stay where you saved them). Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Not medical advice

PlainSpeak is a communication tool. It is not medical advice and does not replace an assessment by a speech and language professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
