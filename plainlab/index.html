PlainLab privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainLab Android app (“PlainLab”), a blood test and lab result tracker. In short: your records stay on your phone unless you turn on sync between your phones. PlainLab has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells data. If you turn on Read with AI, the pages of a report that hold results are sent to Google Gemini through our server to read the values; they are kept in our server's 14-day log, then deleted automatically. The app never asks for access to your location or contacts.

Information the app handles

  • Profiles you create (for example yourself and family members): a name.
  • Lab reports you add: exam date, lab or hospital name, notes, biomarker names, values, units, reference ranges, and the report photos or PDFs you attach.
  • Follow-up checkup reminders and your settings, such as the preferred unit system.

All of it stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else, except the report pages you choose to read with AI (next section) and, if you turn it on, sync between your phones.

Reading reports

The app does not read text on your phone. You can type the values of a report yourself, or have the report read with AI.

Read with AI (optional)

The first time you scan a report, the app asks whether to read reports with AI. If you choose Enter values myself, nothing is ever sent and you type the values. If you choose Use AI (you can switch this off any time in Settings), then for each report you scan:

  • What is sent: the images of the report's pages, as whole pages, including any name, date of birth, ID number or barcode printed on them (you can leave pages out before they are read); the names and units of tests you added yourself, so the same test from another lab is matched to it; and a random install number used only to count the daily AI reads (it is not linked to you, your device or any account). The PDF file itself, your other reports, profiles and notes are not sent.
  • Who receives it: our server, which passes the pages to Google Gemini (a service provider) to read the test names, values, units, reference ranges, date and lab name, and returns them to the app. It is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • What is kept: our server keeps the full request, including the page images, and the answer in its log for 14 days, then deletes them automatically (see “14-day server log” below).
  • What AI does not do: it does not interpret your results, diagnose or give advice. You check and can edit every value before saving.

You get 20 AI reads a day (one read covers up to 8 pages); the count resets at midnight UTC. With no internet, after the daily limit, or when the server is busy, the pages are saved with the report and the app reads them with AI later.

Sync between your phones (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When you turn it on, either by signing in with your Google account or with a sync code, your records (reports with their test results, dates, lab names and notes, your profiles such as "Mom", and follow-up reminders) are stored on our server so that your other phones signed in with the same account or code receive them. Page images and PDF files are never synced. With Google sign-in we receive your Google account's email address and a Google account identifier, only to find your records again on another phone; we use the basic sign-in scopes only. Records are sent over an encrypted connection and are not shared with anyone or used for anything else. Turn off sync stops this phone syncing; Turn off and delete from server deletes your records from our server.

14-day server log

Our AI service and our sync server (PlainAPI) run on the developer's own computer. For every request the app sends to either of them, the server keeps a log entry with the full request and the full answer for 14 days. For PlainLab this includes the report page images you read with AI, which hold health data and any name, date of birth, ID number or barcode printed on them, the test names and values read from them, and, if sync is on, your synced records (test results, dates, lab names, notes, profiles and reminders). Each entry also records the time, the app and its version or the AI model used, and the country and network part of your address (never the full IP address). It never keeps Google sign-in tokens, sync codes, API keys or passwords.

With sync on, your synced records stay on our server for sync as described above; the 14-day log is an extra copy that is deleted on its own after 14 days.

The log is kept only to find and fix problems and to improve the quality of results. After 14 days each entry is deleted automatically, and the log is not included in backups. Only the developer can see it, in a private dashboard behind the developer's Google sign-in. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. To have your requests deleted sooner, email tmosby137@gmail.com.

Photos and documents

You pick a report with Android's photo picker or file picker, or take it with your camera app. The app only receives the file you choose. After you save a report, compressed images of the pages used (about 0.3 MB a page) are kept in the app's private storage so you can look back; the original PDF is not copied.

Notifications

The app asks for permission to show notifications only when you set a follow-up reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Exports and backups

A doctor summary PDF, CSV spreadsheet or backup file is created only when you ask for one, and is sent only to the app you pick in the share sheet. Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick.

Ratings

After you have used the app a few times, Google Play may show its standard rating card. That card is provided by Google Play; the app itself sends nothing.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account under their terms; the developer never receives it.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a report or profile in the app deletes it from the app's storage. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. If you turn on sync, your records stay on our server until you choose Turn off and delete from server. Report pages read with AI, and records sent by sync, are also kept in our server's 14-day log and then deleted automatically.

Not medical advice

PlainLab is a record-keeping tool. Its high and low marks come from the reference ranges printed on your report or entered by you. It does not diagnose anything or give medical advice; talk to your doctor about your results.

What we don't do

  • We don't keep report pages read with AI beyond the 14-day server log.
  • We don't sell data or use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't use your location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
