## 🔐 **Privacy Policy for AI Prompt Manager**

## **Overview**

AI Prompt Manager is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles your data and what information we do and do not collect.

**Key Privacy Principles:**
- **Local Storage Only:** All your data stays on your device
- **No Data Transmission:** We never send your data to external servers
- **No Tracking:** We don't track your browsing or usage patterns
- **No Analytics:** We don't collect usage statistics or analytics
- **Complete User Control:** You own and control all your data

---

## **Information We Do NOT Collect**

### **Personal Information**
- We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers
- We do not require user accounts or registration
- We do not collect demographic information

### **Browsing Data**
- We do not track which websites you visit
- We do not monitor your browsing history
- We do not collect information about your online activities
- We do not track how you use other websites or applications

### **Usage Analytics**
- We do not collect usage statistics
- We do not track how often you use the extension
- We do not monitor which features you use
- We do not collect performance metrics

### **Device Information**
- We do not collect device identifiers
- We do not access system information
- We do not collect browser information beyond what's necessary for functionality

---

## **Information We Store Locally**

All data created by AI Prompt Manager is stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API. This includes:

### **Prompt Data**
- **Prompt text:** The actual content of prompts you save
- **Prompt titles:** Names you assign to your prompts
- **Prompt descriptions:** Optional descriptions you add
- **Categories:** Category assignments for organization
- **Creation dates:** When prompts were created
- **Usage counts:** How many times you've used each prompt (stored locally only)

### **Settings and Preferences**
- **Display preferences:** How you prefer the interface to appear
- **Default categories:** Your preferred default category settings
- **Auto-sort preferences:** Whether you enable automatic sorting

### **Important Notes About Local Storage:**
- All data remains on your device and is never transmitted anywhere
- Data is stored using Chrome's encrypted storage system
- You can export your data at any time through the extension's options page
- You can delete all data at any time through the extension's options page
- Uninstalling the extension will remove all stored data

---

## **How We Use Your Data**

### **Functionality Only**
Your locally stored data is used exclusively to provide the extension's functionality:

- **Prompt Management:** Storing and organizing your saved prompts
- **Quick Access:** Displaying your prompts in the popup and context menus
- **Smart Sorting:** Organizing prompts based on your usage patterns (calculated locally)
- **Export/Import:** Allowing you to backup and restore your prompt library

### **No External Use**
- Your data is never sent to our servers or any third-party services
- We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purposes
- We do not share your data with any external parties
- We do not analyze your data for business intelligence or product improvement

---

## **Permissions Explained**

### **Why We Need Each Permission:**

**Storage Permission:**
- **Purpose:** Save your prompts and settings locally on your device
- **Data Access:** Only data you explicitly create within the extension
- **Privacy Impact:** None - data never leaves your device

**Active Tab Permission:**
- **Purpose:** Detect the current website to optimize prompt insertion
- **Data Access:** Only the URL of the active tab when you use the extension
- **Privacy Impact:** Minimal - only accessed during active use, not stored or transmitted

**Scripting Permission:**
- **Purpose:** Insert prompts into text fields on web pages
- **Data Access:** Ability to interact with text inputs on the current page
- **Privacy Impact:** None - only used for prompt insertion, no data collection

**Context Menus Permission:**
- **Purpose:** Provide right-click menu options for saving and inserting prompts
- **Data Access:** Selected text when you choose to save it as a prompt
- **Privacy Impact:** None - only processes text you explicitly choose to save

**All URLs Permission:**
- **Purpose:** Work on any website where you want to use prompts
- **Data Access:** Ability to interact with text fields on any website
- **Privacy Impact:** None - despite broad access, no data is collected or transmitted
