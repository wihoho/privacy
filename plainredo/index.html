PlainRedo privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainRedo Android app (package com.wihoho.plainredo), a mistake notebook that parents use with their children: you photograph marked homework, the wrong questions are saved in a notebook on the phone, and each comes back to redo on a memory curve. In short: your notebook stays on your phone. Homework photos leave the phone only when you run the AI check, question text only when you ask for a similar question, and the notebook only when you turn on sync between phones. PlainRedo has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and we never sell or share data.

Information the app handles

  • Photos of homework, worksheets and tests that you scan, pick or import from a PDF, and the question crops cut from them.
  • The questions, your child's answers, the right answers, explanations, notes, subjects and topics.
  • Review history (when each question was redone and whether it was right, shaky or wrong), the names you give your children's notebooks, and your settings.

All of this is stored on the phone, in the app's private storage. Handwriting removal, printing and backups happen on the phone.

The AI check (only when you use it)

Before the first check the app asks for your consent. When you press Find wrong answers, the page photos you chose (resized to about 1600 pixels) are sent over HTTPS to our AI gateway, which runs on the developer's own computer and passes them to Google's Gemini model to find the wrong questions and write short explanations. When you press Make a similar question, the question's text, subject and topic are sent the same way. The photos and text, and the answer, are kept in our server's log for 14 days, then deleted automatically (see “14-day server log” below). A random install id (made on the phone, not linked to you) is sent to apply the daily limit of 20 AI requests per phone. You can always mark questions yourself instead, and nothing is sent.

Sync between phones (optional, off until you turn it on)

When you turn on sync in Settings (by signing in with Google or with a sync code), PlainRedo sends your notebook over HTTPS to our sync server, PlainAPI, which runs on the developer's own computer: the children's notebook names, the questions with your child's answers, the right answers, explanations and notes, the review history and schedule, and the question photos (the cropped question and its erased copy, made smaller). Full page photos are not synced. The server keeps them in your family's private sync space, so that your other phone can show the same notebook, together with the time each record changed. Questions you delete are removed from the server too (a short deletion marker is kept so your other phones delete them as well).

With Google sign-in, PlainRedo asks Google only for the basic sign-in permissions (name, email address, account id). The sign-in token is sent to our sync server, which checks it with Google and stores your Google account id and email address next to your sync space, so the same account finds the same notebook on another phone. It is never used for anything else. If you use a sync code instead, anyone who has the code can see and change the notebook, so keep it to your family. "Stop syncing" on a phone stops it there; the notebook stays on the phone. To have your sync space deleted from the server, contact us (below).

14-day server log

Our AI gateway and our sync server (PlainAPI) run on the developer's own computer. For every request the app sends to either of them, the server keeps a log entry with the full request and the full answer for 14 days. For PlainRedo this includes the homework page photos sent for the AI check (they may show a child's name or handwriting), the question text sent for similar questions, the answers that come back, and, if sync is on, the synced notebook records and question photos. Each entry also records the time, the app and its version or the AI model used, and the country and network part of your address (never the full IP address). It never keeps Google sign-in tokens, sync codes, API keys or passwords.

With sync on, your notebook stays on our server for sync as described above; the 14-day log is an extra copy that is deleted on its own after 14 days.

The log is kept only to find and fix problems and to improve the quality of results. After 14 days each entry is deleted automatically, and the log is not included in backups. Only the developer can see it, in a private dashboard behind the developer's Google sign-in. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. To have your requests deleted sooner, email tmosby137@gmail.com.

Children

PlainRedo is made for parents. It does not ask for a child's personal details; a child's notebook can have any name or none. Homework photos may show a child's name or handwriting: page photos are only sent when the parent runs the AI check, and are kept only in the 14-day server log; with sync on, the cropped question photos are also kept in the family's sync space.

Permissions

Internet (for the AI check and the optional sync between phones) and notifications (for the optional daily reminder, asked only when you turn it on). Pages are scanned with Google Play services' document scanner and picked with the phone's photo picker and file picker, so the app needs no camera or storage permission. No location, contacts or microphone permissions.

Backups and deletion

Back up writes one file wherever you choose; it contains your notebook and photos, so keep it safe. Settings → Delete everything removes all records and photos from the phone; uninstalling the app does the same. Without sync, nothing is kept on our server beyond the 14-day server log. With sync on, contact us to have your sync space deleted.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't keep your page photos beyond the 14-day server log, and we don't use your photos to train anything.
