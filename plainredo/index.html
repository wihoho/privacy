PlainRedo privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the PlainRedo Android app (package com.wihoho.plainredo), a mistake notebook that parents use with their children: you photograph marked homework, the wrong questions are saved in a notebook on the phone, and each comes back to redo on a memory curve. In short: your notebook stays on your phone. Homework photos leave the phone only when you run the AI check, and question text only when you ask for a similar question. PlainRedo has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and we never sell or share data.

Information the app handles

  • Photos of homework, worksheets and tests that you scan, pick or import from a PDF, and the question crops cut from them.
  • The questions, your child's answers, the right answers, explanations, notes, subjects and topics.
  • Review history (when each question was redone and whether it was right, shaky or wrong), the names you give your children's notebooks, and your settings.

All of this is stored on the phone, in the app's private storage. Handwriting removal, printing and backups happen on the phone.

The AI check (only when you use it)

Before the first check the app asks for your consent. When you press Find wrong answers, the page photos you chose (resized to about 1600 pixels) are sent over HTTPS to our AI gateway, which runs on the developer's own computer and passes them to Google's Gemini model to find the wrong questions and write short explanations. When you press Make a similar question, the question's text, subject and topic are sent the same way. The photos and text are used for that request only and are not stored on our server (it keeps no copy of requests or answers). A random install id (made on the phone, not linked to you) is sent to apply the daily limit of 20 AI requests per phone. You can always mark questions yourself instead, and nothing is sent.

Children

PlainRedo is made for parents. It does not ask for a child's personal details; a child's notebook can have any name or none. Homework photos may show a child's name or handwriting: they are only sent when the parent runs the AI check, and are not stored on the server.

Permissions

Internet (for the AI check only) and notifications (for the optional daily reminder, asked only when you turn it on). Pages are scanned with Google Play services' document scanner and picked with the phone's photo picker and file picker, so the app needs no camera or storage permission. No location, contacts or microphone permissions.

Backups and deletion

Back up writes one file wherever you choose; it contains your notebook and photos, so keep it safe. Settings → Delete everything removes all records and photos from the phone; uninstalling the app does the same. Nothing about you is kept on our server, so there is nothing there to delete.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't keep your photos or use them to train anything.
