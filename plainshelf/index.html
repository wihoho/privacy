PlainShelf privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy covers the PlainShelf Android app (“PlainShelf”), a book organizer for your home library. In short: your library stays on your phone. PlainShelf has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data. The only thing that leaves the phone is the optional book-details lookup described below.

Information the app handles

  • The books you add: titles, authors, ISBNs, series, shelves, tags, ratings, notes and read status.
  • Cover images you photograph or that the app downloads for a book.
  • Names you type when you lend a book, and the lending dates.
  • Your settings, such as sort order and when you last made a backup.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Book details lookup (optional)

When you scan or type an ISBN, or search for a book by title, PlainShelf asks the free Open Library catalogue (openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive) for the book's details and cover. The request contains only the ISBN or the words you searched for. No account, name, contact, device identifier, location or library contents are sent. Like any website, Open Library receives your phone's IP address with the request; see the Internet Archive's privacy terms. Requests use HTTPS.

You can turn this off in Settings → “Look up details online”. Everything else in the app keeps working; scanned books are saved with their ISBN and you can type the details yourself.

Camera

PlainShelf asks for the camera permission when you first scan a barcode or take a photo of a cover. Barcodes are read on the phone (Google's ML Kit barcode model, bundled in the app). Camera images are not stored, except a cover photo you choose to keep, and are never uploaded.

Backups and spreadsheets

A backup or CSV export is written only where you choose to save it (for example your Downloads folder, a memory card, or a cloud drive you pick in Android's save dialog), or shared to the app you pick. Android's own device backup, if you have turned it on, may also include the app's data under Google's terms; PlainShelf does not control or read it.

Storage and deletion

Deleting a book in the app deletes it from your library. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the phone. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
