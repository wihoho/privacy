PlainCutout privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the PlainCutout Android app (“PlainCutout”), a cut-and-paste photo editor. In short: PlainCutout does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

Information the app handles

  • Photos you choose with Android's photo picker, to cut out or use as a background.
  • The cutouts and pictures you make.

Cutouts are kept in the app's private storage so you can paste them again. None of this is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

PlainCutout asks for no photo, storage or camera permission. You pick photos with the system photo picker, which only gives the app the photos you select. Pictures you save are added to your gallery (Pictures › PlainCutout) through Android's media store, which needs no permission.

Automatic cutout

The “Auto” tool uses a small model (U²-Net, Apache License 2.0) bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

Sharing

Pictures leave your phone only when you choose to share them, and only to the app you pick.

Storage and deletion

You can delete any cutout, or all of them at once, under My cutouts or in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all its data. Pictures you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal or sensitive information.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
